# Security configuration\n\nThe anti-raid/anti-nuke protection is enabled by default. The bot must have **View Audit Log**, **Kick Members**, and **Send Messages/Embed Links** in log channel `1550564287129456810`.\n\nCommands (server owner or Administrator):\n- `انترايد` — show the active protection rules.\n- `تراست @user` or `تراست @role` — exempt a user/role from anti-raid and anti-nuke punishment.\n- `انتراست @user` or `انتراست @role` — remove the exemption.\n\nProtection thresholds:\n- 10 member kicks/bans in 60 seconds.\n- 20 bulk-deleted messages in 20 seconds.\n- 3 deleted channels or roles in 60 seconds.\n- 3 role/permission updates in 60 seconds.\n\nWhen an unauthorized bot is added, the bot and the person who added it are kicked when Discord permissions and role hierarchy allow it. All actions are sent as a readable embed to the configured security log channel.\n
